You should start by writing a first version of a literature review of your selected topic (The selected topic does not have to be related to the chosen seminar paper). The literature review should cover recent work in the selected topic (at least 14 references), and include the identification of challenges in that field., including the one you plan to address (it can evolve in the final report).
Objective
The goal of the survey is to help you better understand the research problem you are working on and gives you an opportunity to get new ideas how to address your project, but also support you in making informed decisions on the methodology you will end up using for your final results. For this task, you need to do some in-depth reading of relevant resources on the internet and/or in the UoA library. This will involve finding articles that describe a problem and/or approaches to solving a problem. You should prefer published peer-reviewed papers and book chapters over blogs and non-peer-reviewed manuscripts where possible. You should map the current problems in the topic you are working on; what approaches have already been used and what are their strengths and weaknesses. Given a number of different approaches, you may be able to organize them along several dimensions, which can give your insight into what possible approaches can be developed that have not yet investigated. The weaknesses are especially important as these should give you suggestions about where you might be able to make a contribution. Regarding the latter, pay attention to the related work section in the papers, and sections that could be named as "Discussion", "Conclusions" and "Future Work".
Requirements and rubrics
The draft should be written in IEEE format. It should be uploaded as a .pdf file of 4 pages. The content of your litterature review should fit in the 4 pages, but references can overflow to a fifth page. 

The review will be graded as follows:
[3%] Relevant discussion of at least 14 references related with the topic (7 per team member).
[1%] Relevant identification of challenges in the field and a specific one you plan to address with your project.
[1%] Style of the survey. This is a first version, but it should still be well written and structured! We expect a short introduction of the context in a few sentences, a discussion of the chosen references and how they can be articulated together, and a short discussion/conclusion including main challenges you identified and the one you plan to address.
