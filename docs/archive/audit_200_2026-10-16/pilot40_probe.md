# Pilot against the framing shortcut: cue vocabulary in forty malicious documents (D-20261016-48)

Probe: the audit's naive Bayes (section 3 of audit200_report.md), twin against malicious, query plus non-target sentences, unigrams and bigrams over selfcheck.normalise tokens, Laplace alpha 1, five folds grouped by family_id (seed 764), no punctuation features. audit.py runs the whole audit at import and needs two command-line paths, so it cannot be imported cleanly; the probe code was copied unchanged into pilot40_probe.py (beside this file) and run as `PYTHONPATH=src python docs/archive/audit_200_2026-10-16/pilot40_probe.py . <output.json>`. The baseline (81.5%) differs from the audit's 81.2% because the reopening (D-20261016-47 b) changed twin sentences after the audit ran.

Reading against D-20261016-48 (d): the overall figure did not fall (it rose by a quarter of a point, within fold noise), and the forty edited families' within-family ranking moved by one family. Two or three cue words in one unrelated sentence do not move a bag-of-words model whose evidence for the twin is spread over the whole cue sentence and the query. This does not meet the decision's reading of a clear fall (five points or more), so under D-20261016-48 (d) the set stands and the 81 percent is reported as the lexical floor, subject to the independent reader's label check (c).

## Selection

Per batch, the two families with the lowest out-of-fold probability that the malicious record is the twin (probe_before.json), excluding the 18 hard negatives (malicious context containing a selfcheck.SECURITY_TOPIC token, the M16 list) and excluding families whose malicious document (query and sentences) already contains two or more of the twin cue's words of four letters or more. The cue is the twin sentence(s) located by selfcheck._cue_sentence_ids, or the twin query for query-position families. Function words of four letters or more (that, this, with, from, into and the like) were not counted as cue words. Batch 18 has only one family that passes the two-word rule; pilot_0175 (two shared words, coordinator and told, the next easiest) was taken to keep two per batch. Cue words placed in an edit avoid role-naming words (translate, English, warning, policy, quote), the M16 tokens, frame words such as copied or kept, and words tied to the target's own topic.
